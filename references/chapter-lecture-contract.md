# Chapter Lecture Contract

Use this for each chapter-level explanation or note.

## Required Shape

### Chapter Role

Explain why this chapter exists in the book. Do not summarize first. Locate the chapter in the whole.

### Before This Chapter

State what the reader should already understand from earlier chapters.

### Central Move

Name the chapter's main intellectual move:

- defines a term
- proves a claim
- introduces a method
- attacks an assumption
- gives evidence
- shifts from theory to practice
- handles objections

### Professor Explanation

Teach the chapter in plain Chinese:

1. What question is the chapter answering?
2. What does the author say?
3. How does the author support it?
4. What example makes it vivid?
5. What detail should not be lost?

### Key Concepts

For each concept:

- author-specific meaning
- simple explanation
- example
- contrast with nearby concepts
- source anchor

### Evidence And Examples

List evidence, cases, stories, data, or arguments used in the chapter. Separate book evidence from outside explanation.

### Common Misunderstandings

Include at least two misunderstandings for dense chapters.

### Beginner Takeaway

State what a complete beginner should remember.

### Advanced Question

State what an advanced reader should challenge, verify, or compare with other views.

### Connection Forward

Explain how this chapter prepares the next chapter or later parts.

## Style Rule

Do not make every chapter sound equally important. Tell the user which chapters are load-bearing and which are supporting, illustrative, or transitional.
