# Book Map Contract

Produce this before durable chapter lectures or method extraction.

## Required Sections

### 1. Book Identity

- Title
- Author
- Edition/year if known
- Source file or source basis
- Reader level assumed in this map

### 2. Core Question

State the main question the book tries to answer. Phrase it as a real problem, not a topic.

Bad: "This book is about productivity."

Good: "How can a knowledge worker protect deep thinking when modern work fragments attention?"

### 3. One-Sentence Thesis

Compress the book into one sentence. If this cannot be done, the map is not ready.

### 4. Structural Spine

List 3-7 major parts. For each:

- role in the book
- chapters included
- relationship to other parts: foundation, extension, contrast, application, critique, case evidence

### 5. Chapter Map

For every chapter:

- chapter title or inferred heading
- what problem it solves
- main claim
- key concepts
- source anchor
- why it matters to the whole

### 6. Concept Dependency Graph

List the concepts that must be learned before others. Use a simple dependency list or Mermaid graph when useful.

### 7. Argument Spine

Extract the main reasoning chain:

```text
Premise -> supporting evidence -> intermediate claim -> conclusion -> implication
```

### 8. Inclusion And Exclusion Boundary

Say what the book covers and what it does not cover.

### 9. Limits And Critique

Include at least three:

- historical or technological limitation
- author background or incentive blind spot
- missing counterexample
- unsupported assumption
- strongest opposing view

## Readiness Check

The book map is complete only if:

- the thesis is one sentence
- the major parts form a coherent structure
- chapter purposes are not generic summaries
- limitations are specific
- important claims have source anchors
